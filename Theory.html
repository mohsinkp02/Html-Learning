*Attributes:
    -attributes are nothing but properties of tag.
    -attribute are used to give the extra information to the tags.
    -attributes must be injected inside the opening tag.
    -attributes are written in name="Value" format(  ).
    -multiple attribute can be written inside the opening tag by separating with space and they are different types of attributes 
    1.Global attribute
    2.Local attribute
    3.Boolean attribute

    1.Global attribute:
        -global attributes can be used in any tag.
        -ex.style,id,class etc.
       
    2.Local attribute:
